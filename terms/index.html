1. About GEMS

GEMS (Girls in Engineering, Mathematics, and Science) is a student-led organization based at Chadwick International in Songdo, Incheon, South Korea. We share free STEM resources: workshops, the OPAL textbook, GEMS Lab experiment videos, and the Hidden GEMS podcast. The website is free to use.
