Initiatives

Four paths.
One mission.

GEMS works on STEM equity in two directions: breadth, by covering everything from calculus to synthetic biology, and depth, by breaking hard ideas like wave-particle duality down into stories and experiments anyone can follow. Each initiative below is one way we do that.

01

Hidden GEMS

Conversations with the women shaping science, told in their own words.

A podcast where women scientists, professors, and PhDs talk about how they got into their fields, what they're working on, and what it's actually like to be a woman in STEM today.

Learn more
02

OPAL

Observe. Problem-solve. Apply. Lead.

A 100+ page STEM textbook and companion experiment kit for ages 10–14, covering eleven topics from quantum physics to neuroscience. Already in classrooms in Kenya and Rwanda, and now on Amazon.

Learn more
03

GEMS Lab

Experimental science is not rich science.

A free physics video series built around 37 experiments you can do with household materials, each paired with a full printable lab worksheet. Built to turn students into researchers, not spectators.

Learn more
04

In-Person Workshops

Hands-on STEM days at children's centers across Korea.

Multi-day workshops at children's centers and libraries, bringing experiments, equity, and a bit of wonder to students who rarely get to do science with their hands.
