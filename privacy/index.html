1. Who we are

GEMS (Girls in Engineering, Mathematics, and Science) is a student-led organization based at Chadwick International in Songdo, Incheon, South Korea. We run gems-jk.org, where you can read about our work, watch GEMS Lab experiments, listen to the Hidden GEMS podcast, and join the discussion. In this policy, "GEMS", "we" and "us" mean GEMS, and "you" means anyone who visits or uses the website.

2. What we collect

You can browse most of the website without an account. We only collect personal information when you create an account, comment, or contact us.

  • Account information. When you create an account with email, we collect your email address, a password (stored only as a protected hash by our sign-in provider, never visible to us), and the name you choose to show on your comments. When you sign in with Google, see section 3.
  • Comments. When you post a comment, we store its text, the name on your account, a private account ID that links the comment to you, the page it was posted on, and the time it was posted. Comments are public: anyone visiting that page can read them, along with your display name.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, use the contact form, or ask to host a workshop, we receive your name, email address, and whatever you write (for workshop requests, also your organization, city, preferred dates, and expected number of students).
  • Technical information. Our sign-in and database provider (Google Firebase) automatically processes basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to keep accounts secure and the service running.

We do not collect your phone number, address, date of birth, payment details, or location, and we do not run advertising or analytics trackers on the website.

3. Google sign-in

If you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares the following with us, only after you agree on Google's screen:

  • your name, which becomes the display name on your comments;
  • your email address, which identifies your account;
  • your Google account ID and profile picture link, which our sign-in provider receives. We do not display your picture.

We use this information only to sign you in, show your name on comments you post, and contact you about your account if needed. We do not ask for access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google data, and we never post anything to your Google account.

GEMS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or let humans read it except where needed for security, to comply with the law, or with your permission.

4. How we use information

  • to create and run your account and keep you signed in;
  • to show members-only content (GEMS Lab videos and Hidden GEMS episodes) to signed-in members;
  • to display your comments with your name, and let you delete them;
  • to reply to messages you send us;
  • to keep the website safe, including removing spam or harmful comments.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that run the website for us, and only as much as they need:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores accounts and comments. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Drive hosts our videos and podcast episodes. When a signed-in member plays one, it loads from Google Drive, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to that player.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website's files and, like any web host, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver pages. GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers messages from our contact and workshop forms to our email inbox. It receives what you type into the form. FormSubmit privacy policy.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our members and others.

6. Where it's stored and for how long

Comments are stored in Google Cloud's Seoul region (South Korea). Account sign-in data is managed by Google Firebase, which may process it in other countries, including the United States, under Google's safeguards.

  • Comments stay until you delete them, until we remove them, or until your account is deleted.
  • Accounts stay until you ask us to delete yours. We may also delete accounts that have been inactive for more than two years.
  • Messages to us are kept only as long as needed to respond.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. To keep you signed in, our sign-in provider saves a small amount of data in your browser's storage. If you sign out or clear your browser data, it's removed. The Google Drive player may set its own cookies when a video plays; see Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Students and children

GEMS is made for students, and some of our visitors are young. You can watch our public pages without an account.

  • If you are under 14, please ask a parent or legal guardian before creating an account. Korean law requires guardian consent to collect personal information from children under 14.
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has created an account without your permission, email us and we will delete the account and its comments.
  • We encourage everyone, especially younger members, never to post personal details such as a home address, phone number, or school schedule in comments.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete a comment at any time with the "Delete" button under it.
  • Delete your account. Email us from the address on your account and we will delete the account and all your comments within 30 days.
  • See or correct your information. Email us and we'll tell you what we hold about you and fix anything that's wrong.
  • Remove Google access. You can disconnect GEMS from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Depending on where you live, including under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act, you may have further rights, such as asking us to stop processing your information. Contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

10. Security

Passwords are handled by Google Firebase and never seen or stored by us. All traffic to the website is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules only let people post as themselves and delete their own comments. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will tell affected members promptly if a breach occurs.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how we use your information, we'll also post a notice on the website before it takes effect.
