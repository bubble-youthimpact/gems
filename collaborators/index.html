Collaborators

We don't do this alone.

The organizations and people who work with GEMS to make STEM education accessible across genders and borders.

GEMS is student-run, and we're honest about what that means: we move fast, we're close to the students we serve, and we have limits. Our collaborators fill them. They bring professional networks, physical presence in places we can't be every week, and the kind of institutional perspective that tells us whether what we're building actually works. This page is who they are and what we do together.

Work with GEMS.

We partner with professional societies, schools, nonprofits, companies, and individuals who want to widen the pipeline into STEM. Collaborations range from a single podcast episode to co-running a workshop series or distributing OPAL in a new country.
